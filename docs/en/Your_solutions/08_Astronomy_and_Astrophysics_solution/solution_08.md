How much time would it take to get from Earth to Mars when Mars is at its closest approach (55 million km)

a) for a message sent at the speed of light?

b) by a spacecraft traveling at a constant speed of 40,000 km/h = 11.11 km/s?

c) by "airplane" traveling at a constant speed of 900 km/h, typical for intercontinental flights?

---

To calculate the travel time for each scenario, we use the basic kinematics formula for time:

$$\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}$$

**Given Distance:** $55\text{ million km} = 55,000,000\text{ km} = 5.5 \times 10^7\text{ km}$ (or $5.5 \times 10^{10}\text{ m}$)

---

### a) A message sent at the speed of light

Radio waves and light travel through space at the speed of light ($c$).

* **Speed ($c$):** $\approx 300,000\text{ km/s}$ ($3 \times 10^5\text{ km/s}$)

$$\text{Time} = \frac{55,000,000\text{ km}}{300,000\text{ km/s}} \approx 183.33\text{ seconds}$$

Converting to minutes:


$$\frac{183.33}{60} \approx \mathbf{3.06\text{ minutes}} \quad (\text{or } 3\text{ minutes and } 4\text{ seconds})$$

---

### b) A spacecraft traveling at a constant speed of $40,000\text{ km/h}$

This speed is similar to the escape velocity achieved by the Apollo missions.

* **Speed:** $40,000\text{ km/h}$

$$\text{Time} = \frac{55,000,000\text{ km}}{40,000\text{ km/h}} = 1,375\text{ hours}$$

Converting to days:


$$\frac{1,375\text{ hours}}{24\text{ hours/day}} \approx \mathbf{57.29\text{ days}}$$

---

### c) An "airplane" traveling at a constant speed of $900\text{ km/h}$

Assuming a commercial airliner could somehow fly through the vacuum of space at its normal cruising speed.

* **Speed:** $900\text{ km/h}$

$$\text{Time} = \frac{55,000,000\text{ km}}{900\text{ km/h}} \approx 61,111.11\text{ hours}$$

Converting to days and years:


$$\text{Days} = \frac{61,111.11}{24} \approx 2,546.3\text{ days}$$

$$\text{Years} = \frac{2,546.3}{365} \approx \mathbf{6.98\text{ years}}$$

---

### Summary Table

| Mode of Travel | Speed | Travel Time (at closest approach) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Radio/Light Message** | $300,000\text{ km/s}$ | **~3 minutes** |
| **Fast Spacecraft** | $40,000\text{ km/h}$ | **~57 days** |
| **Commercial Airplane** | $900\text{ km/h}$ | **~7 years** |

*(Note: In actual space flight, spacecraft don't travel in a straight line between planets because both Earth and Mars are moving in their own orbits. Real missions use a curved path called a Hohmann transfer orbit, which typically takes about 7 to 9 months).*
