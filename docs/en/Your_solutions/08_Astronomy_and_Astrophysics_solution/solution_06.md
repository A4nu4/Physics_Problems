Calculate the acceleration due to gravity on the surface of the Sun. By what factor would your weight increase if you could stand on its surface? (Sun's mass $M_S \approx 2 \times 10^{30}$ kg; Sun's radius $R_S \approx 6.96 \times 10^8$ m).
---

To calculate the acceleration due to gravity on the surface of the Sun, we use Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation, which determines the gravitational field strength of any spherical celestial body.

---

### 1. Calculate the Sun's Surface Gravity ($g_{\odot}$)

The formula for surface gravity is:

$$g_{\odot} = \frac{G M_S}{R_S^2}$$

#### Given Constants:

* **Gravitational Constant ($G$):** $6.674 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$
* **Mass of the Sun ($M_S$):** $2.0 \times 10^{30} \text{ kg}$
* **Radius of the Sun ($R_S$):** $6.96 \times 10^8 \text{ m}$

#### Calculation:

Substitute these values into the formula:

$$g_{\odot} = \frac{(6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \cdot (2.0 \times 10^{30})}{(6.96 \times 10^8)^2}$$

$$g_{\odot} = \frac{1.3348 \times 10^{20}}{4.84416 \times 10^{17}}$$

$$g_{\odot} \approx 275.55 \text{ m/s}^2$$

---

### 2. Determine the Weight Increase Factor

Your weight is directly proportional to the acceleration due to gravity ($W = mg$). To find the factor by which your weight would increase, we compare the Sun's surface gravity to Earth's surface gravity ($g_{\oplus} \approx 9.81 \text{ m/s}^2$):

$$\text{Factor} = \frac{g_{\odot}}{g_{\oplus}} = \frac{275.55 \text{ m/s}^2}{9.81 \text{ m/s}^2} \approx 28.09$$

---

### Final Result:

* **Acceleration due to gravity on the Sun:** Approximately **$276 \text{ m/s}^2$** (or $275.6 \text{ m/s}^2$).
* **Weight Increase Factor:** Your weight would increase by a factor of roughly **$28$ times**.

For perspective, if you weigh $70\text{ kg}$ (about $154\text{ lbs}$) on Earth, you would weigh nearly $2,000\text{ kg}$ (about $4,300\text{ lbs}$) on the Sun. The immense crushing force of your own weight would make it impossible to stand, even setting aside the extreme temperature!
