What is the escape velocity from the surface of the Moon? (Moon's mass $M_M \approx 7.35 \times 10^{22}$ kg; whereas Moon's radius $R_M \approx 1,737$ km). Express the result in km/s and as a fraction of Earth's escape velocity (Earth's escape velocity $\approx 11.2$ km/s).
---

To find the escape velocity from the surface of the Moon, we use the formula derived from the conservation of energy, where the initial kinetic energy of an object must balance its gravitational potential energy at an infinite distance.

---

### 1. Calculate the Moon's Escape Velocity ($v_e$)

The escape velocity formula is:

$$v_e = \sqrt{\frac{2GM_M}{R_M}}$$

#### Given Starting Values:

* **Gravitational Constant ($G$):** $6.674 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$
* **Mass of the Moon ($M_M$):** $7.35 \times 10^{22} \text{ kg}$
* **Radius of the Moon ($R_M$):** $1,737 \text{ km} = 1.737 \times 10^6 \text{ m}$

#### Calculation:

Substitute these values into the formula to get the velocity in meters per second ($\text{m/s}$):

$$v_e = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \cdot (7.35 \times 10^{22})}{1.737 \times 10^6}}$$

$$v_e = \sqrt{\frac{9.81078 \times 10^{12}}{1.737 \times 10^6}}$$

$$v_e = \sqrt{5.6481 \times 10^6} \approx 2,376.57 \text{ m/s}$$

Convert this to kilometers per second ($\text{km/s}$):

$$v_e \approx 2.38 \text{ km/s}$$

---

### 2. Express as a Fraction of Earth's Escape Velocity

Now we compare the Moon's escape velocity to Earth's escape velocity ($v_{\text{Earth}} \approx 11.2 \text{ km/s}$):

$$\text{Fraction} = \frac{v_{\text{Moon}}}{v_{\text{Earth}}} = \frac{2.38 \text{ km/s}}{11.2 \text{ km/s}} \approx 0.2125$$

This can be written as a decimal fraction (**$\approx 0.21$**) or simplified close to a standard fraction:

$$\text{Fraction} \approx \frac{1}{4.7} \approx \frac{15}{71} \approx \frac{1}{5}$$

---

### Final Result:

* **Moon's Escape Velocity:** **$2.38 \text{ km/s}$**
* **Fraction of Earth's Escape Velocity:** Approximately **$0.21$** (or roughly **$\frac{1}{5}$** of Earth's escape velocity).

This relatively low escape velocity explains why the Moon cannot retain a significant atmosphere; gas molecules heated by the Sun easily exceed this speed and escape into space.
