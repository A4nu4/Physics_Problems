A measurement of time is recorded as $t = 5.45 \pm 0.22$ seconds. What is the percentage uncertainty of this measurement?

---

To find the percentage uncertainty of the measurement, we express the absolute uncertainty as a percentage of the measured value.

### The Percentage Uncertainty Formula

$$\text{Percentage Uncertainty} = \left( \frac{\text{Absolute Uncertainty}}{\text{Measured Value}} \right) \times 100\%$$

**Given Values:**

* **Measured Value ($t$):** $5.45\text{ s}$
* **Absolute Uncertainty ($\Delta t$):** $0.22\text{ s}$

---

### Calculation

Substitute the values into the formula:

$$\text{Percentage Uncertainty} = \left( \frac{0.22}{5.45} \right) \times 100\%$$

$$\text{Percentage Uncertainty} \approx 0.040366 \times 100\%$$

$$\text{Percentage Uncertainty} \approx 4.04\%$$

---

### Final Result:

The percentage uncertainty of the measurement is approximately **$4.04\%$**.
