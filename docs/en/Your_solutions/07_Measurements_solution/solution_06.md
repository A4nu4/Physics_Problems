A digital thermometer reads $25.4^\circ\text{C}$. Assuming the uncertainty is half the value of the last digit, what is the absolute uncertainty of this measurement?

---

The last digit displayed on the thermometer is in the tenths place ($0.1^\circ\text{C}$).

### Calculation:

If the uncertainty is half the value of this last digit, we calculate it as:

$$\text{Absolute Uncertainty} = \frac{0.1^\circ\text{C}}{2} = 0.05^\circ\text{C}$$

---

### Final Result:

The absolute uncertainty of the measurement is **$0.05^\circ\text{C}$**.

Written in standard scientific notation, the complete measurement is expressed as **$(25.40 \pm 0.05)^\circ\text{C}$**.
